## Changed

- `packages/document/src/cacheexport.ts` (new): `exportAssembly` exports one live assembly, or `undefined` if a
  member is not `valid`, has no geometry or has a broken ref, or the kernel refuses mid-export.
- `packages/document/src/document.ts`:
  - `DocumentOptions.geometryCacheEnvelope` defaults to `geometryCache.current`.
  - `#commit` queues built roots and forgets the export of any root rebuilt, restored or deleted.
  - `exportCacheIdle(max)` keys each export on landing and drops it if an edit replaced the live geometry.
  - `geometryCacheFiles()` is synchronous and calls no kernel op. It recomputes keys, writes idle exports with
    a matching key, and carries restored or never-built roots from the file, never built ones.
- `cacherestore.ts`: `usableRecord` (key and shape, shared by restore and carry) and `recordEntries`.
- `bnn.ts`: `GeometryCacheReader.readMany`, one zip pass with the same per-entry caps; `read` = `readMany([n])`.
  A 16k carry through `read` would be O(n²).
- `tests/geometry-cache-export-d29.test.ts` (new, real OCCT, R3's fixture: 5 assemblies, 20 solids): 8 tests,
  plus 1 env-gated 16k measurement.
